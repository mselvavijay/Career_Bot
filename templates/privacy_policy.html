Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Applies to HireKrkeDekho (hirekrkedekho.vercel.app)

1. Who We Are

HireKrkeDekho is an AI-powered career platform for tech job-seekers in India — personalised job matching, 3-week prep roadmaps, and a daily AI quiz. Reach us at hirekrkedekho@gmail.com.

We do not collect payment info, national IDs, GPS location, or biometrics.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Authenticate you via Google and maintain your session
  • Provide AI career consultation (via Groq / Llama) with your profile as context
  • Persist consultation history across visits
  • Display your Daily Contest score on the leaderboard using your display name (not email)
  • Enforce fair-play (one attempt per day per account)
We never sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, recruitment agencies, or data brokers.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In: Shares your name, email, and picture with us on sign-in. We verify the token server-side and never store your password. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • Groq / Llama AI: Your consultation messages are sent to Groq to generate AI responses. Your email is never shared with Groq.
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL): Database hosted in EU region, encrypted at rest and in transit. Supabase Privacy ↗
  • Vercel (hosting): Serverless infrastructure. Access logs retained per Vercel policy ↗. No tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & correct: Open your Profile modal to view and edit all stored data.
  • Delete: Click "Delete My Account" in the Profile modal to permanently erase your profile, sessions, contest records, and identity. Irreversible.
  • Export: Email hirekrkedekho@gmail.com to request a data export.
  • Leaderboard opt-out: Your display name (not email) appears publicly. Race data auto-purges after 7 days. You can skip the Daily Contest at any time.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
