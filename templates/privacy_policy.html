Privacy Policy & Data Retention

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Effective date: October 2026
This policy applies to HireKrkeDekho (hirekrkedekho.vercel.app) and governs how we collect, use, store, and protect your data.

1. Who We Are

HireKrkeDekho is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that helps job-seekers in India with personalised career consultation, role matching, interview preparation, and daily AI quizzes. We are operated by an independent team and can be reached at hirekrkedekho@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

Category Examples How collected
Account identity Google name, email address, profile picture URL, Google user ID Google OAuth 2.0 sign-in
Candidate profile Degree, skills, years of experience, target role, location, current CTC, expected CTC You fill in the Profile modal

We do not collect payment information, national identity numbers, precise GPS location, or any biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Authenticate you securely via Google and maintain your session
  • Provide personalised career consultation powered by the Groq / Llama AI model, with your profile as context
  • Store and retrieve your consultation history so you can continue sessions across visits
  • Display your Daily Contest score on the public leaderboard (using your chosen display name, not your email)
  • Enforce fair-play rules (one attempt per day per account) in the AI Race
  • Generate anonymised analytics to improve the platform (no individual profiling)
  • Send transactional emails only if you contact us directly via Gmail
We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers, recruitment agencies, or data brokers.

5. Google Authentication & Third-Party Services

Google Sign-In: We use Google Identity Services (GIS) to authenticate you. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us. We verify the Google ID token on our server and never store your Google password or refresh token. Google's privacy policy governs data held on Google's side — see policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Groq / Llama AI: Your consultation messages are sent to the Groq API to generate AI responses. Groq's data processing agreement governs this transfer. We do not share your email or account identity with Groq.
  • Supabase: Our PostgreSQL database is hosted on Supabase (EU region). Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Vercel: Our web application is hosted on Vercel's serverless infrastructure. Vercel may collect access logs. See vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Cookies & Browser Storage

  • Session token (career_bot_token): Stored in localStorage. Contains a signed JWT with your Google user ID, email, and name. Used to authenticate API requests. Cleared on sign-out.
  • Google GIS cookies: Google's sign-in library may set its own cookies (e.g. g_state) governed by Google's cookie policy.
  • No tracking cookies: We do not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics.
You can clear all site data from your browser settings at any time (Settings → Privacy → Clear site data). This also signs you out.

7. Your Rights & Controls

  • Access: Sign in and open your Profile modal to view everything stored about you — degree, skills, experience, and account identity.
  • Correction: Edit any profile field in the Profile modal at any time.
  • Deletion (Right to Erasure): Click "Delete My Account" in the Profile modal to permanently delete your candidate profile, all consultation sessions, contest records, and account identity from our database. This is irreversible.
  • Data portability: Contact us at hirekrkedekho@gmail.com to request an export of your stored data.
  • Opt-out of AI Race leaderboard: Your display name (not email) appears on the public leaderboard. Race data is automatically purged after 7 days. You can skip the Daily Contest at any time.

8. Security Measures

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS 1.3
  • Database stored on Supabase with row-level security (RLS) and encryption at rest
  • JWT tokens are signed with a secret and validated server-side on every API request
  • Admin endpoints are protected by a secret key and IP-level rate-limiting (30 requests per 60 seconds)
  • No plaintext passwords are ever stored — authentication is entirely delegated to Google
  • Production schema changes use Alembic migrations — no ad-hoc DDL on live databases
If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to hirekrkedekho@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

HireKrkeDekho is designed for job-seekers aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has provided us personal data, we will delete it immediately. Contact us at hirekrkedekho@gmail.com if you believe a minor's data has been submitted.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. Continued use of HireKrkeDekho after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

For material changes that significantly affect your rights, we will display a notice on the home page for at least 14 days before the change takes effect.
