The Three Pillars of Your Assessment

Rather than generic chatbot answers, CareerBot evaluates three mandatory candidate parameters together to calculate the ideal career match:

1. Degree & Education

Evaluates your academic foundation, qualifications, and degree discipline (B.Tech, M.S., BCA, MCA, or cross-domain conversions) to establish eligibility for specialized roles.

2. Years of Experience

Strictly matches job seniority. Freshers and students (0 yrs) receive entry-level and junior roles, while experienced candidates receive senior, lead, or staff architect positions.

3. Technical Skills

Your technical competencies directly define your target domain and job categories (e.g. Python, SQL, React, PyTorch, Docker), eliminating guesswork.
